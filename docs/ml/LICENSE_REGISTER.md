## Approval checklist for a new asset

- Record the exact source URL, version/revision, checksum, and retrieval date.
- Confirm image provenance and whether commercial evaluation/training is permitted.
- Confirm redistribution, derivative-weight, and attribution obligations.
- Store datasets outside Git and never commit customer or policyholder media.
- Freeze vehicle-grouped train/validation/test manifests before tuning thresholds.
- Record per-class results and representative failures, not only aggregate scores.
